The supplied primary keyword was blank. “Competitive exam preparation online” was selected because the homepage covers both government and entrance examinations. “Government exam preparation” should primarily belong to /government-exams/, reducing keyword cannibalization.

1. SERP and Search-Intent Findings

Live web research was conducted for India-focused queries. Exact personalized Google India rankings, search volumes, complete autocomplete data and a canonical People Also Ask feed were unavailable; no rankings or volumes have been invented.

Dominant intent

  • Find an online competitive-exam preparation platform.
  • Select a target examination.
  • Access mock tests, previous-year questions, study material and current affairs.
  • Evaluate whether a platform suits the aspirant’s exam and preparation stage.

Secondary intent

  • Learn how to begin preparing.
  • Find free resources.
  • Compare preparation platforms.
  • Improve weak subjects, speed and accuracy.
  • Find official notifications, eligibility and dates.
  • Explore courses, coaching and test series.

Competitor landscape

Representative results included Testbook, Oliveboard, Adda247, Unacademy, PW, MockSutra and OnlyMock.

Common competitor patterns include large exam catalogues, mock-test entry points, PYQs, current affairs, language filters, analytics, courses, pricing, testimonials and app promotion.

Common questions

  • How should a beginner start preparing?
  • Which platform is best for government exams?
  • Can government exams be prepared for online?
  • Are mock tests and previous-year papers available?
  • Which exams does the platform cover?
  • How do mock tests improve speed and accuracy?
  • Can SSC, Banking and Railway exams be prepared for together?
  • Is material available in Hindi or regional languages?
  • What are the course and test-series fees?

Information-gain opportunities implemented

  • A concise beginner preparation method
  • Guidance for choosing an exam
  • Separate paths for beginners and stalled candidates
  • Clear distinction between Brolly content and official recruiting-body information
  • Official links to SSC, UPSC, IBPS and Indian Railways
  • Clearly labelled sample analytics
  • A demo conversion path without fabricated outcomes
  • Removal of unverified testimonials and time-sensitive status cards

Validation checklist

  • Primary keyword appears naturally in the title, H1 and main content.
  • Government-exam intent links to /government-exams/.
  • Mock tests, PYQs, study material, current affairs and analytics are covered.
  • SSC, UPSC, IBPS and Indian Railways relationships are explicit.
  • Beginner, free-resource, suitability, process and objection queries are covered.
  • Homepage and supporting-page intent separation reduces cannibalization.
  • Exactly one H1 renders on the homepage.
  • Organization, WebSite and WebPage schema match visible facts.
  • No ratings, reviews, prices or LocalBusiness details were invented.
  • Forms have visible labels, native validation and focus states.
  • Question answers are visible without clicks.
  • Responsive black, white and yellow design is retained.
  • Production build passes successfully.
